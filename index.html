邀请函 Invitation

为深化中国与北欧经贸务实往来，充分发挥海外华人华侨桥梁作用，促进深圳南山区与瑞典斯德哥尔摩双向产业对接。斯德哥尔摩作为北欧生物医药、绿色产业核心枢纽，南山区作为粤港澳大湾区战略性新兴产业高地，两地在生命健康、生物医药、绿色低碳、循环经济、数字工业等领域具备广阔合作空间。

由深圳市南山海外联谊会主办，深圳市加中创新中心有限公司承办，北欧深圳总商会、北欧创新中心协办，举办深圳南山-瑞典斯德哥尔摩生命健康产业经贸交流会，谨诚挚邀请您出席本次盛会。

本次交流会汇集中瑞政府代表、高校专家、行业机构及生命健康领域重点企业，开展两地营商环境与产业政策推介，分享产业发展实践，搭建企业对接协作平台，深度挖掘双向投资与商业合作机遇，助力两地企业拓展北欧与中国市场。

To deepen practical economic and trade cooperation between China and Northern Europe, and drawing on the bridging role of overseas Chinese communities, we aim to promote two-way industrial cooperation between Nanshan District, Shenzhen and Stockholm, Sweden. Stockholm is a core Nordic hub for biomedicine and green industries, and Nanshan is a high ground for strategic emerging industries in the Guangdong–Hong Kong–Macao Greater Bay Area. The two regions share broad potential in life and health, biomedicine, green and low-carbon development, the circular economy and digital industry.

Organized by the Shenzhen Nanshan Overseas Friendship Association, co-organized by SCI Innovation Centre Co., Ltd., and supported by the Nordic Shenzhen Chamber of Commerce and Innovation Centre Northern Europe, the Shenzhen Nanshan – Stockholm Life and Health Industry Economic and Trade Exchange Conference will be held in Stockholm. We cordially invite you to attend.

The conference brings together Chinese and Swedish government representatives, academic experts, industry organizations and leading companies in life and health. It will present the business environment and industrial policies of both regions, share industry practice and build a business matching platform, opening up opportunities for two-way investment and commercial cooperation as companies expand into the Nordic and Chinese markets.
